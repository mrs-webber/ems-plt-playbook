### Agenda

| Time | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 min | Review team norms. |
| 5–18 min | Baseline the team on the Collaborative Team Rubric — rate silently first, then compare and agree. Add the readiness / Critical Issues self-assessment. |
| 18–30 min | Discuss patterns: Where are we already functioning well? Where are we stuck? |
| 30–40 min | Identify one area of focus — including one rubric descriptor to move up a level by the next check-in. |
| 40–45 min | Choose the course/unit area the team will use for this year's cycle. |

### Why This Meeting Matters

Teams cannot improve what they have not named. This meeting gives teams permission to be honest without judgment.

The Collaborative Team Rubric is the mirror — a baseline the team will beat, not a grade. It stays with the team; it is not reported up. Expect to be "emerging" in places — that is the whole point. Score it honestly now, re-score it at a buffer meeting and again at Meeting 18, and the team gets to watch itself level up. It's the same move teams made with their norms: turning the vague question "are we a good team?" into something specific and observable.
