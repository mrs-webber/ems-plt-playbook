> **Buffer meeting.** No new content — this is a reinforcement and correction meeting. The first intentional pause built into the year.

### Agenda

| Time | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 min | Revisit any confusion from Meetings 1–3. |
| 10–25 min | Finalize your team foundation: refine the roles and norms from Meeting 2, and complete the Holding Each Other Accountable worksheet if it was a first pass. |
| 25–40 min | Readiness check: use the Four Critical Questions readiness tool to name where the team is ready for the cycle and where it still needs support. |
| 40–45 min | Decide what standards and materials everyone brings to Meeting 5, where the standards work begins. |

### Why This Meeting Matters

This is the first intentional buffer. Some teams will still be confused, skeptical, or waiting to be told exactly what to do. This meeting prevents teams from moving forward without clarity.

It's also where the Meeting 2 foundation gets finished: refine your roles and norms and complete the accountability worksheet, so the way your team works is settled before the learning cycle begins.

Then the team looks forward: the Four Critical Questions readiness tool gives an honest read on where you're ready for the cycle and where you still need support — so no one moves into the content work pretending. The standards work itself begins next time, at Meeting 5.
