> **Buffer meeting.** No new content — this is a reinforcement and correction meeting. The first intentional pause built into the year.

### Agenda

| Time | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 min | Review team norms and roles, then revisit any confusion from Meetings 1–3. |
| 5–25 min | Finalize your team foundation: refine the roles and norms from Meeting 2, and complete the Holding Each Other Accountable worksheet if it was a first pass. |
| 25–40 min | Work the readiness checklist on the back of the agenda: check off every task from Meetings 1–4, and use any remaining time to finish anything that isn't done yet. |
| 40–45 min | Decide what standards and materials everyone brings to Meeting 5, where the standards work begins. |

### Why This Meeting Matters

This is the first intentional buffer. Some teams will still be confused, skeptical, or waiting to be told exactly what to do. This meeting prevents teams from moving forward without clarity.

It's also where the Meeting 2 foundation gets finished: refine your roles and norms and complete the accountability worksheet, so the way your team works is settled before the learning cycle begins.

Then the team confirms it's ready: work through the readiness checklist on the back of the agenda, check off every task from Meetings 1–4, and use the remaining time to close any gaps — finishing incomplete foundation work is exactly what this buffer is for. The four critical questions aren't self-assessed here; teams meet them as the cycle unfolds, and give the honest, experience-based read at the end of the year (Meeting 18). The standards work itself begins next time, at Meeting 5.
