### Why This Meeting Matters

When teams begin looking at common student evidence, the work can feel vulnerable. Norms help protect the work and keep the conversation focused on learning rather than blame.

This meeting sets up the whole way your team works — roles first, then norms, then how you'll hold each other to them — as three short worksheets that lead into each other. Treat it as a strong first pass: you'll finalize and refine at the Meeting 4 buffer.
