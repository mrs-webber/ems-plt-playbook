### Agenda

| Time | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 0–8 min | Review the full learning cycle from essential standard to reassessment. |
| 8–20 min | Complete team reflection, including a final re-score of the Collaborative Team Rubric — compare to your Meeting 3 baseline. |
| 20–30 min | Identify what worked, what was hard, and what should be adjusted next year. |
| 30–38 min | Choose one PLC practice the team wants to strengthen next. |
| 38–45 min | Celebrate completion of the first authentic PLC learning cycle — and how far the team has come on the rubric. |

### Why This Meeting Matters

Culture changes when teams pause to recognize what they learned and how their practice changed. This meeting helps teams see that PLC is not a meeting structure. It is a continuous improvement process.

Close the loop on the team, too: this is the final Collaborative Team Rubric re-score. Set it beside the Meeting 3 baseline and the mid-year check, and let the team see — in specific, observable terms — how far it has come.
